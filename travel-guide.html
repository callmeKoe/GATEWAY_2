Myanmar Travel Guide

Destinations, culture, food and tips

Famous Tourist Destinations

Bagan

Over 2,000 ancient temples on a vast plain. Best seen by electric bike or hot air balloon at sunrise.

Inle Lake

Floating gardens, stilt villages and the Phaung Daw Oo Pagoda.

Yangon

Home of the golden Shwedagon Pagoda, Bogyoke Market and colonial downtown.

Mandalay

Royal palace, Mandalay Hill, Mahamuni Buddha and the U Bein teak bridge.

Culture & Traditions

Myanmar is a predominantly Buddhist country with 135 recognised ethnic groups. Visitors will see monks collecting alms at dawn, colourful festivals such as Thingyan (Water Festival) in April, Thadingyut (Festival of Lights) in October and Tazaungdaing in November. Traditional dress includes the longyi, and thanaka paste is worn on the face as sun protection and decoration.

Food & Local Dishes

🍜 Mohinga

Fish noodle soup, the national breakfast dish.

🥗 Lahpet Thoke

Fermented tea leaf salad with nuts and beans.

🍛 Shan Noodles

Rice noodles in a light tomato and chicken broth.

🍲 Ohn No Khao Swe

Coconut chicken noodle curry.

Local Travel Tips

  • Dress modestly at temples, cover shoulders and knees, and remove shoes and socks.
  • Carry some clean US dollar notes and Myanmar kyat; cards are not accepted everywhere.
  • Always ask permission before photographing monks or local people.
  • Drink bottled water and use sunscreen and insect repellent.
  • Check current travel advice and visa requirements before you travel.

Seasonal Travel Advice

Season Months What to expect
Cool season Nov to Feb Best weather, peak tourist season. Book early.
Hot season Mar to May Very hot in central Myanmar; good for beaches and highlands.
Rainy season Jun to Oct Green scenery, fewer crowds, lower prices. Bagan stays relatively dry.
