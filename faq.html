Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a visa to visit Myanmar?

Most visitors need a visa or an online visa. Rules change, so check the official government site before you travel.

What is the best time to visit?

November to February is the coolest and most popular period. See our Travel Guide for seasonal advice.

Can I customise a tour?

Yes. Contact us with your dates and interests and we will build a private itinerary.

What is your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 30 days before departure. Later cancellations may incur a fee.

Is Myanmar safe for tourists?

Conditions vary by region. We only operate in areas currently open to tourists and follow official advice.
